\begin{abstract}
We study thin plates where out of plane deformations are activated by wedge disclinations which are configurations observed    in metal plates and graphene.

...We are interested in the situation where out-of-plane deflections are activated by membrane kinematic incompatibility,
noticeably, Volterra's disclinations. We show existence of equilibrium solutions in the presence of a finite system of disclinations and a dead load for the deflection under various boundary conditions. We set up a numerical code in Fenics environment for the resolution of the equilibrium equations which we study in a set of parametric test studies. Our implementation of the Finite Element method follows the Discontinuous Galerkin Method with $C^0$ elements.
 \end{abstract}
